# Prototype*

`index.html` is the clickable sumday daily prototype: the morning feed, filters, hearts, jam drawer, vision board and the sumday dashboard. Open it in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo and visit `/prototype/`.

\* A proposal. Rebuild it in sumday's design language. Friends, prices, dates and tips are demo data.

## Media credits

Every photo and video in `media/` comes from Wikimedia Commons under a Creative Commons licence or in the public domain.
